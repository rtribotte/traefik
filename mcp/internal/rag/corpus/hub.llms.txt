- [crd.managedapplication](https://doc.traefik.io/traefik-hub/api-management/managed-applications.md): ManagedApplication. ManagedApplication represents a managed application.
- [crd.managedsubscription](https://doc.traefik.io/traefik-hub/api-management/managed-subscription.md): ManagedSubscription. ManagedSubscription defines a Subscription managed by the API manager as the result of a pre-negotiation with its
